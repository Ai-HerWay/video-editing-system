The palette in practice
Espresso and ink provide depth. Linen and ivory give the grid air. Dusty blue is a signature field; olive and sage add richness. Walnut supports photographic tones. Gold is a fine detail, never a body-text colour.
Use ink on cream or blue; ivory on espresso or olive. Avoid low-contrast sage text, faint labels, sepia washes and rust.
The recognisable details
One expressive serif headline. One italic word or short phrase. Clean sans-serif supporting copy. A small brand label. An optional thin gold line.
Photography brings texture. Layout brings restraint. One collage device per composition is enough.

05 / Photography & layering
Life around the work.
Tailoring & texture
Brown, cream, natural fabric, a close crop. Use for confidence, clarity and intentional work.
Space & ease
Soft daylight, generous negative space, natural work moments. Preserve the airy whites.
Green & presence
Deep green, sculptural shapes, restrained accessories. Let the image carry the colour.
Layer order
Photo → local contrast wash → optional paper or cutout → headline → supporting line → brand and page furniture. Keep all typography editable. Place graphics around the subject; avoid covering eyes, hands or the meaningful object.
Image generation brief
Editorial lifestyle photography; natural directional light; warm neutrals with cool blue or olive accents; tactile fabric and stone; realistic proportions; intentional negative space for text. No lettering, logos or invented founder likeness. Use supplied stock before generating more.

A flexible nine-post rhythm
This composition study mixes photo, blue, cream, green, opinion and proof. Judge the balance across the whole nine, rather than forcing a checkerboard.
At least one light or mid-tone tile in each row.
No identical photograph within the visible nine.
Use founder images selectively, usually two or three per nine when suitable originals are available.
Keep blue, cream and green substantial; cap dark covers at roughly four per nine.
Try two or three proof-led covers per twelve posts when there is genuine proof to share.

09 / Make it repeatable
The designer & AI handoff.
Before production
Define the audience, one idea and the promised payoff.
Choose a named cover family and real source assets.
Write the hook, optional supporting line and one CTA.
Map the carousel sequence or Reel first-frame promise.
Before publishing
Check type, contrast, clipping and thumbnail legibility.
Check crop, face and object placement.
Check surrounding nine for tonal balance and duplicates.
Verify every claim, screenshot, quote and attribution.
Export stills as PNG; keep editable source and copy with versioned filenames.
Reusable instruction
Create an AI Her Way Instagram asset using this design system. First specify: audience, one idea, hook, optional supporting line, content type, cover family, source assets, and intended action. Use Playfair Display + Montserrat, the exact palette, real supplied photography, editable text and restrained editorial graphics. Build carousel slides at 1080×1350 and Reel covers at 1080×1920. Check the hook at thumbnail size, complete the promised payoff, preview Reel crops, and check the surrounding nine for image repetition and tonal balance. Do not fabricate evidence or founder identity. If an authentic screenshot or review is missing, leave a clearly labelled placeholder. Return the content, layout choices, source list and final QA result.
