07 â€” Look book Â· photography

Grade A Â· espresso (dark covers)
Warm the shadows, cool the highlights a touch so timber does not tip sepia, drop the brightness, soft radial vignette. Grade the photo darker so cream type has something to sit against. Never wash it out with a cream veil.
