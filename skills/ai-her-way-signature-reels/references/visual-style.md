# Approved visual direction

Sophisticated, warm, visibly crafted. Presenter-led teaching with expressive editorial headings, animated hand-drawn explanations and meaningful evidence. The approved example is a small hybrid edit; its exact duration and number of stickers are not requirements for all reels.

## Brand and typography

Use the palette and starter dimensions in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

- Advercase Regular for titles and one-word speech captions; genuine Advercase Italic for selected heading emphasis. Montserrat supports compact labels and metadata. The pilot also uses Advercase for short diagram labels.
- Commercial Advercase font files are intentionally not bundled. The editor needs licensed local Regular and Italic files; reference them through project-relative asset paths. If unavailable, request the licensed files or an explicitly approved substitute before claiming a brand-matched final export. Do not silently revert to Playfair.
- Ordinary speech captions are one word at a time, accurately timed, with a steady anchor and an Espresso backing where needed. No forced keystroke, pop or bounce per spoken word. Questions, quotes and featured text are separate designed elements and may reveal by typing.
- Preserve natural letter proportions. Use a neutral pair plus one accent per scene as a starting point; add another accent where it explains a meaningful contrast.
- Linen/Ivory surfaces, Espresso/Ink lettering and Walnut pen strokes define the paper stage. Dusty Blue and Sage give stickers distinct roles. Avoid small low-contrast Gold text on Linen.

## Layout for vertical reels

Approved pilot: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Adapt frame rate to the project and source; do not treat upscaled footage as newly captured detail.

Caption starting box: x=75, y=1340, width=810, roughly 105 px high, 76 px Advercase. This raised placement leaves space below for the handle and post text and beside the right-hand control rail. Review long words and multiple-line featured text independently.

Approximate review reserves: top 120 px; right of x=915; below y=1460. These are project-specific guides, not official universal Instagram safe zones. Do not publish a mock interface as part of the reel. Protect real moving faces, hands and evidence as well as UI clearance; move the caption anchor at a sensible phrase or shot boundary if necessary.

## Stickers and drawings

Use relevant hand-drawn objects and infographics, not arbitrary decorative icons. The pilot's document, workflow and completed-document stickers explain a connected system. Future illustrations must fit the new subject.

- Modestly irregular die-cut Ivory rims, brand-coloured paper fills, open ink drawings and restrained soft shadows.
- Rounded stroke caps/joins, starting at about 3–5 px on a 1080-wide canvas. Imperfect curves give a human pen character; avoid continuously boiling outlines.
- Brief spring arrival with bounded overshoot, a small rotation and a clear landing. Preserve the same sticker identity when it moves onto the paper stage.
- Build connections in explanatory order. Start → Connect → Finish is this pilot's story, not a universal diagram template. A feedback arrow visibly returns to its destination.
- Headings can use fine drawn underlines. Their sound is selective: the approved V5 pilot has silent underlines; do not restore the rejected extra scribble layer automatically.

## Motion and pacing

Let each visual clarify the spoken thought. Alternate purposeful builds with readable holds and presenter moments. Use a paper slide or travelling element to bridge a scene change; do not assign every cut a whoosh.

Pilot timings are examples: sticker entrance 0.47 s with a soft spring, diagram relocation 0.68 s with slight stagger, paper entry 0.55 s, connector draw 0.42 s, long feedback draw 1.15 s. Scale timing to complexity and speech rather than stretching this 9.6-second sequence over a full reel.

Questions and quotes must hold long enough to read after the reveal. Show real screenshots as evidence, and clearly distinguish illustrative diagrams from actual product results. Match footage with a restrained warm grade while keeping skin natural; graphics retain their exact brand colours.
